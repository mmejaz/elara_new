# Getting Started

> Signing in and finding your way around.

## Signing in

1. Open the app in your browser. You'll land on the **Login** page.
2. Enter your **email** and **password** and select **Sign in**.
3. On success you're taken to the **Dashboard**.

If your email or password is wrong, you'll see *"The provided credentials are
incorrect."* — the same message either way, for security. After several failed
attempts in a row you may be temporarily blocked; wait a minute and try again.

> There is no public sign-up. Accounts are created for you by an administrator
> (see [Managing Users](users.md)). A "Forgot password?" link exists but
> self-service reset isn't enabled yet — ask an administrator to set a new
> password for you.

## The screen at a glance

- **Sidebar (left):** your navigation menu. It's grouped into sections
  (Management, Account, Lookups, and so on). Click a group to expand it. You only
  see the items your account is allowed to open.
- **Header (top):** your organization selector, theme and display settings, and
  your account menu.
- **Main area:** the current screen.

## Switching organization

If your setup uses multiple organizations, an **organization switcher** in the
header sets the one you're currently working in. Some lists (like Departments)
change with the selected organization — see [Organizations](organizations.md).

## Appearance

Open the **settings** control in the header to adjust the look and feel — light or
dark theme, accent color, compact spacing, and text size. Your choices are
remembered on this device.

## Signing out

Use your account menu in the header and choose **Log out**. If your session
expires while you're away, the app returns you to the Login page automatically.
