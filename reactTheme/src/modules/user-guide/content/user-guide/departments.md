# Departments

> **Where:** Lookups → All Lookups → Department

Departments are the teams or divisions within your organization. Like
organizations, they can be arranged as a **hierarchy** (a parent department with
sub-departments).

## What you can do

- See departments in a list.
- Add a department, optionally under a **parent department**.
- Edit or delete a department.

## Add a department

1. Select **Add Department**.
2. Enter a **name**.
3. Optionally pick a **parent department** to nest it. Leave empty for a
   top-level department.
4. Depending on how your workspace is configured (see below), you may also choose
   which **organization** the department belongs to.
5. Select **Save**.

## Shared, scoped, or flexible

How departments relate to [organizations](organizations.md) depends on a
workspace-level setting your administrator controls:

- **Shared** — departments are common to all organizations.
- **Scoped** — each department belongs to one organization; you'll see only the
  departments for the organization selected in the header.
- **Flexible** — a mix: a department can be shared, or tied to a specific
  organization.

If your department list changes when you switch organizations in the header, your
workspace is using **scoped** (or **flexible**) mode.

## Edit or delete

- **Edit:** open the row, change the name, parent, or organization, then **Save**.
- **Delete:** remove a department; reassign or remove its sub-departments first.

## Tips

- Not seeing a department you expect? Check the **organization switcher** in the
  header — the list may be scoped to another organization.
