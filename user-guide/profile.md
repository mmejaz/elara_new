# Your Profile

> **Where:** Account → Profile

Your Profile is where you manage your own account. It has three tabs.

## Personal Information

Your name, email, phone, and designation, plus your **profile picture**.

- **Edit details:** change any field and select **Save**.
- **Profile picture:** upload an image to set your avatar; you can replace or
  remove it at any time. It appears in the header and around the app.

## Security

Change your **password** here.

1. Enter your **current password**.
2. Enter and confirm your **new password**.
3. As you type, a checklist shows the requirements turning green — your password
   must have **at least 8 characters**, with **upper- and lower-case letters, a
   number, and a symbol**.
4. Select **Update password**. You'll stay signed in.

## Access

A read-only view of **what you're allowed to do**:

- Each **role** you hold, and the permissions that role grants.
- Any **permissions assigned directly** to you (outside a role).

If you're missing access you expect, this tab shows exactly what you currently
have — share it with an administrator when asking for a change. See
[Roles & Permissions](roles-and-permissions.md) for how access is granted.
